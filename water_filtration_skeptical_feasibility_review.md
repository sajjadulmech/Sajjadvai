## 3. Market Entry & YMYL (Your Money, Your Life) Friction

Because water filtration touches public health, Google applies elevated **E-E-A-T scrutiny**.

```
OVERCOMING THE YMYL BARRIER (The Solo Founder Protocol)
├── 1. The "Engineering Systems" Positioning (Focus on hardware, flow, and physics—not medicine)
├── 2. Primary Scientific Citations (Link every contaminant claim directly to EPA / CDC / NSF data)
├── 3. NSF/ANSI Standard Cross-Referencing (Verify claims against official public NSF/WQA databases)
└── 4. Transparent Testing Protocol (Document real TDS meters, pressure gauges, and drain timers)
```

### Actionable Rules to Pass Google's Quality Raters
1. **No Medical Prescriptions:** Never write *"Drink RO water to cure arthritis"*. Write *"Reverse osmosis removes 98% of dissolved minerals, reducing sodium intake according to EPA guidelines"*.
2. **Cite Primary Standards:** Reference specific standards (**NSF/ANSI 42 for taste/odor, 53 for health effects like lead/PFAS, 58 for reverse osmosis**).
3. **Public Test Data Integration:** You do not need a $50,000 spectrometry lab. Use a **$25 calibrated digital TDS meter, a $15 inline water pressure gauge, and a 1-liter graduated cylinder with a stopwatch** to measure real-world flow rates and drain ratios.

## 4. Starting Scope: The Single Beachhead Strategy

Starting with "General Water Quality" is a fatal trap for a solo founder with 10 hours/week. It spreads topical authority too thin.

```
THE BEACHHEAD EVOLUTION PATHWAY
[MONTHS 1–6: THE BEACHHEAD]     --> [MONTHS 7–12: EXPANSION 1]   --> [MONTHS 13–24: EXPANSION 2]
Reverse Osmosis & Drinking Water     Contaminants & Water Testing     Whole-House & Well Water
(Under-Sink, Countertop, TDS)        (PFAS, Lead, City Water CCR)     (Softeners, Iron/Sulfur, GPM)
```

### The Chosen Beachhead: **Option B (Reverse Osmosis & POU Drinking Water Purification)**

#### Why Reverse Osmosis is the #1 Beachhead:
1. **High Search Intent & Clear Boundaries:** Searchers looking for under-sink RO, tankless RO, and remineralization filters have immediate purchase intent.
2. **Manageable SKU Count:** There are ~15 dominant RO brands (Waterdrop, APEC, iSpring, Express Water, Nu Aqua, Clearly Filtered), making hands-on evaluation achievable.
3. **High Direct Affiliate Margins:** Tankless RO systems sell for **$350–$800**, paying **8% to 12% ($35–$96/sale)** through ShareASale/Awin.
4. **Natural Expansion:** Once you dominate RO, expanding into *Contaminants (PFAS/Lead)* and *Whole-House Filtration* is a seamless lateral move.
