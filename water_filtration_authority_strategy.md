### 1.1. SEO & Algorithmic Advantages
* **Topical Map Completeness:** Google's semantic index evaluates whether a domain covers the complete entity graph. A site that only writes "best reverse osmosis" lacks entity nodes for *activated carbon adsorption isotherms, total dissolved solids (TDS), membrane pore size, and feedwater pressure requirements*. A knowledge-first site completes the topical graph, elevating rankings across all commercial queries.
* **Helpful Content System (HCS) Immunity:** Google actively penalizes sites whose primary intent is monetization. A site where 70% of content answers diagnostic and educational questions is classified as an **informational authority**, shielding commercial pages from core update penalties.

### 1.2. Defeating Google AI Overviews (AIO)
* Pure definitions (*"what is reverse osmosis"*) get scraped by AI summaries.
* **What AI cannot steal:**
  1. *Empirical test datasets:* Measuring pure-to-drain wastewater ratios ($1:1$ vs $3:1$) under varying real-world household PSI ($40\text{ PSI}$ vs $75\text{ PSI}$).
  2. *Interactive diagnostic logic:* Inputting well-water test numbers (e.g., $3.5\text{ ppm Iron}, 7.2\text{ pH}, 18\text{ GPG Hardness}$) to output precise system sizing calculations.
  3. *Engineering teardowns:* Internal valve cross-sections, O-ring seal tolerances, and booster pump durability curves.

### Real Technical Differentiators to Showcase

1. **Fluid Dynamics & Flow Sizing:**  
   Explain why an undersized 1" whole-house filter causes severe shower pressure drops when the lawn irrigation turns on. Use real flow formulas ($Q = C_v \sqrt{\Delta P}$).
2. **Micron Pore Physics:**  
   Explain why nominal 5-micron filters allow cryptosporidium cysts to bypass, whereas absolute 1-micron or $0.0001$-micron RO membranes guarantee log-4 ($99.99\%$) pathogen reduction.
3. **Membrane Thermodynamics:**  
   Explain why an RO system rated for $100\text{ GPD}$ at $77^\circ\text{F}$ produces only $48\text{ GPD}$ in the winter when tap water drops to $45^\circ\text{F}$. (Lifestyle bloggers never mention temperature compensation factors!).
4. **Corrosion & Galvanic Reactions:**  
   Warn homeowners why aggressive pure RO water ($0\text{ TDS}, \text{acidic pH}$) will corrode copper piping if plumbed directly into unlined copper refrigerator lines.
