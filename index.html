The 3-Step Purity Framework

An engineering-first approach to choosing, sizing, and maintaining water filtration.

01

Chemical Testing

Never buy a filter without precise chemical data. Test for GPG hardness, iron, TDS, MDCL, PFAS, and lead using certified standards.

02

System Sizing

Match flow rates (GPM), pressure drops, resin capacities, and NSF standards to your specific household demand.

03

Verification

Validate post-installation purity with long-term TDS sensors, cartridge micron checks, and subsequent annual profiles.
