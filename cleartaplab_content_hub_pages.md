3. **Remineralization & Alkaline Post-Filters:** Restoring calcium and magnesium carbonates to optimize drinking pH and taste.
4. **Wastewater Recovery Ratios:** Evaluating 1:1, 2:1, and 3:1 pure-to-drain efficiency and drain line pressure requirements.
5. **Membrane Maintenance & TDS Creep:** Managing Fickian ion diffusion during idle periods and scheduling cartridge replacements.
